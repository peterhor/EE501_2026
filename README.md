# EE501 - Climate Change and Ecology

Course website for EE501 at the University of South-Eastern Norway: interactive lecture
material for Lectures 1-8 and the three PC labs.

Live site:

**[https://peterhor.github.io/EE501_2026/](https://peterhor.github.io/EE501_2026/)**

Author and maintainer: [Peter Horvath](https://www.usn.no/kontakt-oss/ansatte/peter-horvath-1),
University of South-Eastern Norway
([ORCID 0000-0002-6017-5385](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6017-5385)).

## What is on the site

The site is static HTML served from `docs/`. Its contents:

1. `docs/index.html`: landing page with the course schedule and the Canvas link
2. `docs/lecture-1-components-of-the-climate-system/` through
   `docs/lecture-8-climate-change-the-future/`: the interactive pages for each lecture
3. `docs/pc-lab-1-daisyworld/`, `docs/pc-lab-2-energy-balance-model/`,
   `docs/pc-lab-3-abrupt-vegetation-change/`: each PC lab as a browser model (`index.html`)
   with its tasks and questions on a separate page (`tasks.html`)
4. `docs/equations/`: the equation summary for the course
5. `docs/references.html`: the reference library behind the pages, with DOIs

Each lab runs entirely in the browser and saves your work locally on your own computer. Nothing
you enter is sent anywhere.

## What is not on the site

The lecture slides, the lab report templates and the textbook are distributed through Canvas,
not through this repository. Answer material is not published.

## Credits

The course was originally developed by Hans Renssen, the former course coordinator. The three
lab models are browser versions of his Excel workbooks. The set text is Goosse, H. (2015),
*Climate System Dynamics and Modelling*, Cambridge University Press; the open 2010 edition is
also cited where the pages follow its numbering. Full details are on the references page.

The interactive pages, the browser lab models and the reference library were built for the
2026 edition.
