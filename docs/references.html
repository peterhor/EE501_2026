Audit result

What this audit established

Most course pages already have a usable source note in the footer. The main gap was discoverability: the literature and DOI trail were spread page by page rather than exposed as a course-level bibliography.

Pages based mainly on first-principles calculations, constants, or textbook equations are still grounded in the local footer notes; this page concentrates on the literature that students may want to open directly.

Where a DOI below is present, it was checked directly against Crossref from this workspace. Only verified items are exposed on this student-facing page.

Audit Notes

  • All 46 audited teaching pages with source-note footers had a defensible scientific basis. The dominant issue was that DOI-level references were not available in one place.
  • The most complete paper-backed sourcing is already present in Lecture 2 budget pages, Lecture 4 carbon-cycle pages, Lectures 7-8 paleoclimate and future-scenarios pages, and the provenance appendix.
  • The main follow-up work is bibliographic completion, not scientific rescue: books, chapter-derived demos, and abbreviated legacy citations should be expanded carefully rather than filled from memory.
