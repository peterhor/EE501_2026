How to read this page

Every DOI below was resolved against Crossref from this workspace, and every report or dataset link was checked to return a live page. Nothing here is filled in from memory: where an identifier could not be confirmed, the source is listed under still to confirm rather than given a plausible-looking DOI.

“Cited on” lists the published pages that actually name the source, so you can go the other way — from a paper to the demonstration built on it. Pages resting on first-principles calculation rather than on a paper say so in their own footer, and are not padded with citations here.

Course texts

Two different books by the same author sit behind this course, and they are numbered differently. The 2010 open edition is the one the page-level source notes and equation numbers follow; the 2015 Cambridge book is the set text.

Goosse et al. (2010) — the edition this site follows
Goosse, H., P.Y. Barriat, W. Lefebvre, M.F. Loutre and V. Zunz (2010), Introduction to Climate Dynamics and Climate Modelling. Open online edition.
The figure, equation and section numbers quoted in the grey source note at the foot of each teaching page, and the numbering in Parts I–V of the equation summary, refer to this edition.
